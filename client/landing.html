Security

Encrypted before it ever rests.

  • bcrypt, ten rounds. Passwords are hashed with a configurable cost and never logged, never returned.
  • Tokens die when you do. Access tokens carry a jti bound to a session row; logout flips the switch on every window.
  • Three limiters, globally shared. 100 req / 15 min general, strict 5 on auth, 10 / min bursts - counted in Redis across replicas.
  • Audit trail. Register, login, and logout events land in an audit log with IP and timestamp.
